About Us

A calm place to reset

THERAPYSPA is designed around a simple idea: a clean, quiet, and private environment helps the body and mind fully relax.

Clean, private, and peaceful

Our space is created for guests who want to step away from daily pressure and enjoy a focused moment of care. Soft lighting, tidy treatment rooms, and a quiet atmosphere make every visit feel personal and restorative.


Whether you are coming in after a long workday, a busy week, or simply need time to unwind, THERAPYSPA offers a soothing setting dedicated to deep comfort and relaxation.

FAQs

Before your visit

How early should I arrive?

Please arrive 10 minutes early so you have time to check in, relax, and prepare for your session.

Can I get a massage if I have an injury?

Please call ahead before booking if you have a recent injury, medical concern, or sensitive area. For serious conditions, consult your healthcare provider first.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please call as early as possible if you need to change or cancel your appointment so the time can be offered to another guest.

Is there parking available?

Parking availability may vary nearby. Please call before your visit if you need parking guidance.
