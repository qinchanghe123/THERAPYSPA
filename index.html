FAQs

Before your visit

How early should I arrive?

Please arrive 10 minutes early so you have time to check in, relax, and prepare for your massage or wellness session.

Can I receive the free $100 coupon online?

The $100 coupon benefit is available for guests who visit us in store. Please ask our staff when you arrive.

What is chair massage good for?

Chair massage is a short session designed for quick relief of neck, shoulder, upper back, and daily work-related tension.

What is cupping therapy?

Cupping therapy is a traditional wellness technique often used to support relaxation and help release the feeling of body tension. It is wellness-focused and not a substitute for medical treatment.

Can I get a massage if I have an injury?

Please call ahead before booking if you have a recent injury, medical concern, active bleeding, severe pain, or a sensitive area. For serious conditions, please consult your healthcare provider first.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please call as early as possible if you need to change or cancel your appointment so the time can be offered to another guest.

Is there parking available?

Parking availability may vary nearby. Please call before your visit if you need parking guidance.
